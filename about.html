About Us

An advertising business built around one type of customer: independent car dealerships.

My Story

I've loved cars and creating for a long time, and I've worked on vehicles for multiple years. That hands-on experience shapes the way I think about advertising for dealerships.

Today, Jae's Auto Media runs Facebook and Instagram advertising for independent car dealerships. Independent lots compete against bigger names, and social media gives a local dealership a direct way to show real inventory to shoppers who live nearby.

I handle the campaigns so you can focus on what you do best, which is selling cars. When you work with me you deal with Jae directly, so you get straight answers, quick replies and advertising shaped around your lot instead of a one-size-fits-all package.
