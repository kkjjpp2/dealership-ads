Our Services

Focused ways we put your inventory in front of local buyers, on Facebook, Instagram and Google.

01

Digital Inventory Targeting

Put your specific vehicles in front of active car buyers in your local radius using dynamic social media ads.

  • Ads built around the vehicles you actually have on your lot
  • Targeting set to your dealership's local area
  • Placements on both Facebook and Instagram
  • Ongoing adjustments as your inventory changes

Best for: dealers who want specific vehicles seen by nearby shoppers.

02

Showroom Retargeting

Re-engage people who browsed your inventory but haven't booked a test drive yet.

  • Follow-up ads for people who looked at your vehicles online
  • Reminders about the cars they were interested in
  • A clear next step, like booking a test drive or sending a message
  • Ad frequency kept reasonable so the ads help rather than annoy

Best for: dealers who get steady online interest and want to win back shoppers who haven't come in yet.

03

Lead Generation Campaigns

Capture inquiries straight from Facebook and Instagram so your sales team can follow up while buyers are interested.

  • Inquiry options right inside Facebook and Instagram
  • Contact details sent to you so your team can respond quickly
  • Campaigns aimed at inquiries and appointments, not just views
  • Adjusted over time based on what is bringing in the best leads

Best for: dealers who want more inquiries and appointments on the books.

04

Targeted Google Ads

Put your dealership at the top of Google when local shoppers search for the vehicles you sell.

  • Search ads that appear when buyers look for cars near you
  • Targeting set to your dealership's local area
  • Clear calls to call, get directions or view your inventory
  • Budget and keywords adjusted over time based on what brings in real customers

Best for: dealers who want to reach buyers at the moment they are actively searching.

Not sure which fits?

Tell us about your lot and we'll recommend a plan that makes sense for your dealership.
